Video understanding · Multimodal learning

Ruixiang Zhao 赵瑞祥

Ph.D. Candidate / Renmin University of China

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate at AIMC-Lab, School of Information, Renmin University of China, advised by Prof. Xirong Li. I received my B.Eng. degree from Renmin University of China in 2022.

My research focuses on video understanding and multimodal learning, with recent work on streaming audio–visual understanding and real-time interaction.
